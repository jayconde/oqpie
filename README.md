Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 2, 2026

This policy explains how Oqpied Studio ("we", "us"), based in Ontario, Canada, handles information in the Lolobee mobile app ("the app").

Short version
Lolobee keeps your inventory on your own device. We do not run accounts, we do not collect your inventory, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Information stored on your device
Everything you enter in the app stays on your device: item names, quantities, notes, categories, properties, photos, receipt photos and saved barcodes. We do not have access to this data and it is not uploaded to our servers. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it.

Camera and photos
The app asks for camera access to scan barcodes and QR tags and to take item and receipt photos. Photos and scans are processed and stored on your device only.

Barcode lookups
For food items (kitchen and pantry), when you scan a barcode the app may send that barcode number to the public Open Food Facts database (openfoodfacts.org) to fill in a product name and brand. Only the barcode number is sent, but that service receives your IP address as part of any internet request. Please see Open Food Facts' own privacy policy for how they handle it. For medicine, the app saves the barcode number on your device only and makes no lookup.

Purchases
Purchases are handled by Apple's App Store or Google Play. We use RevenueCat, a third-party service, to confirm what you have unlocked and to restore purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier and purchase information, and does not receive your name or payment card details. See RevenueCat's privacy policy for details.

Reports and exports
PDF reports and QR tags you create are generated on your device. You decide whether to save, print or share them. If you share them, they are handled by the app you choose.

Analytics and advertising
The app does not include advertising and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Children
The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices
You can remove all data held by the app at any time by deleting it from your device. You can revoke camera or photo permissions in your device settings.

Changes to this policy
If we change this policy we will update the date above. If a future version of the app collects any new information, we will update this policy before release.
